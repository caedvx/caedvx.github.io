About

I’m a developer and co-founder of ODIVM, a Swedish software company. I love designing and analysing systems to find and eliminate inconsistencies and inefficiencies.

My work runs across the whole stack, from web apps and AI agents at ODIVM to native Android, client-side cryptography in C++ and particle-in-cell fusion simulation on the GPU.

Away from the keyboard I’m usually thinking about a new code project, how to make ODIVM products better, playing Battlefront 2, building rockets in Kerbal Space Program, or tinkering with something new.
