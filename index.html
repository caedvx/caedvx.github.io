About

Who I am

A bit about my background and what I work with.

I'm CAEDVX, a developer and co-founder of ODIVM, a Swedish software company. I care about the details: smooth motion, tight design systems, and clean architecture.

I work across the stack, from web apps and AI agents at ODIVM to native Android in Kotlin, end-to-end encryption in C++, and particle-in-cell fusion simulation on the GPU.

When I'm not coding I'm either thinking about UI, design systems, and ways to make software feel premium without getting in the way of usability, playing battlefront 2, building rockets in Kerbal Space Program, or tinkering with new tech and side projects.
